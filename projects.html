LED Logo Sign: i.c.stars Tech Expo

Tech Expo Project · i.c.stars Cycle 60 Internship · Individual project with an i.c.stars advisor · 2026

A Wi-Fi-controlled RGB LED sign of the i.c.stars logo that I designed and built for the Cycle 60 Tech Expo. To make it a shared piece of our cycle, my cohort signed the board itself.

  • Soldered a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W through 3 MOSFET modules to the LED strip and a stripped USB power cable.
  • Wrote MicroPython code in Thonny so visitors can change the sign's colors and effects from a phone.
  • Used a multimeter to troubleshoot the wiring until all 3 colors worked.
  • Solved a power problem: a power bank and a solar panel couldn't supply enough power for the LEDs, so I switched to a 10-ft USB-A cable to power the sign reliably.
  • Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W
  • MicroPython
  • Soldering
  • Circuit Wiring
  • Multimeter Testing
  • LED Strips
